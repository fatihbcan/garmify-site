Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

The short version

  • Your wardrobe stays on your phone. Your clothing images, outfit history, plans and packing lists are stored locally on your device — not on our servers.
  • We do not track you. Garmify contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting and no third-party tracking software of any kind.
  • We never sell your data. Not to anyone, for any purpose.
  • We use an AI provider to tag your garment photos and to suggest outfits. It receives your photos and written descriptions of your clothes and preferences — never your name, email or account identifier.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app, and it really deletes it.

2. What we collect, and why

2.1 Account information

When you create an account we collect your email address, password, first name and surname. Your password is never stored in readable form — it is hashed by our authentication provider, and we cannot see it. We use this information solely to create and secure your account, sign you in, and address you by name in the app.

2.2 Photos of your clothing

When you add a garment, the photo is sent to our AI provider so it can automatically identify what the item is (its name, category, colour, pattern, warmth and formality). This is what saves you from typing those details in yourself.

The photo is transmitted for that single purpose and the resulting description is returned to your device. We do not store your photos on our own servers — the processed image is saved in the app's local database on your phone. Please avoid photographing people, documents or anything sensitive in the background of your garment photos.

2.3 Details used to suggest outfits

To suggest what to wear, the app sends a short written description of the relevant part of your wardrobe and your preferences to our AI provider. Nothing here identifies you — no name, email or account identifier is included — but you should know what it contains:

  • Descriptions of your garments — name, category, colour, pattern and similar tags. The images themselves are not re-sent.
  • A styling note derived from your gender (for example, "menswear"). If you chose "prefer not to say", nothing is sent.
  • Your age as a number — derived from your date of birth. Your actual date of birth is never sent.
  • Your occupation, dress code and any planned activities, so the suggestion suits your day.
  • Any event description you type into the day's plan, and trip destinations and dates when building a packing list.

Because a free-text event description is sent as you wrote it, please avoid putting anything sensitive or identifying in that field.

2.4 Location

Garmify suggests outfits based on the weather, which requires knowing roughly where you are. If you grant location permission, we take your device's position and deliberately round it to roughly one kilometre before sending it to our weather provider to retrieve the local forecast. Your precise coordinates never leave the app. You may instead search for a city by name, in which case that search term is sent rather than your device location.

Location is used only to fetch weather. It is not stored on our servers, not used to build a profile, and not shared with anyone else. You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings; the app remains usable, and you can set your location manually.

2.5 Information stored only on your device

The following is held in the app's local storage and is never transmitted to us or anyone else:

  • Your garment images, including the background-removed versions
  • Your exact date of birth — only your age in years is ever sent, as described above
  • Your outfit history and record of what you have worn
  • Your saved outfits, weekly plans and packing lists
  • Your notification preferences and subscription status

Because this data is local, uninstalling the app or signing out permanently erases it. It is not backed up to us, and we cannot restore it for you.

2.6 Subscription information

If you subscribe to Garmify Pro, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see or receive your payment card, billing address or similar financial details. We only learn whether an active subscription exists.

3. What we do not do

To state it plainly, Garmify does not:

  • include analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting software
  • track your behaviour across other apps or websites
  • build advertising profiles or infer interests for marketing
  • sell, rent or trade your personal information
  • use your photos to train AI models of our own

6. Legal bases for processing

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region with similar law, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract — to create your account and provide the features you asked for, including garment tagging and outfit suggestions.
  • Consent — for device location and for push notifications. You may withdraw either at any time in your device settings, without affecting the rest of the app.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the service secure and functioning, and to respond to your support requests.

7. How long we keep information

  • Account information is kept for as long as your account exists, and is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Photos and descriptions sent to our AI provider are processed transiently to produce tags and suggestions, and are not retained by us. The provider may retain them briefly for abuse-prevention and operational purposes under its own policy.
  • Location data is used in the moment to fetch a forecast and is not retained by us.
  • On-device data remains until you delete it, sign out, or uninstall the app.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of the following rights over your personal data:

  • Access — to obtain a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Rectification — to correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • Erasure — to have your information deleted
  • Portability — to receive your information in a machine-readable format
  • Objection and restriction — to object to, or limit, certain processing
  • Withdrawal of consent — at any time, where we rely on consent
  • Complaint — to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time, from inside the app: open Profile → Delete account and confirm. This permanently removes your account and its associated information from our authentication provider, and erases all Garmify data stored on your device. It cannot be undone. If you would rather we did it for you, email support@fatihberatcan.com from your registered address.

To exercise any other right, contact support@fatihberatcan.com. We will respond within one month.

9. Security

All communication between the app and our providers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and are never visible to us. Your wardrobe data is stored in your device's app-private storage, which other apps cannot read.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affecting your personal data occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authority where the law requires it.

10. Children

Garmify is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 years old to create an account, and if you are under the age of digital consent in your country (which is up to 16 in parts of the European Union) you need your parent or guardian's permission to use it. We do not knowingly collect information from children below these ages. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact support@fatihberatcan.com and we will delete it.

We may update this policy as the app changes. When we do, we will revise the "last updated" date above. If a change materially affects how we handle your personal data, we will give you notice in the app before it takes effect.
