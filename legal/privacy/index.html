Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

The short version

  • Your wardrobe lives on your device and is backed up to your private account. Your clothing images, outfit history, plans and packing lists are kept on your phone and synced to your Garmify account in the cloud, so they come back when you reinstall. Only you can access them.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites. Garmify carries no advertising, no attribution and no cross-app tracking software, and does not use an advertising identifier. It does include Firebase Crashlytics, so we learn when the app breaks, and Firebase Analytics — which is off until you turn it on, and which you can turn back off at any time in Profile → Share usage data.
  • We never sell your data. Not to anyone, for any purpose.
  • We use an AI provider, reached through our own server, to tag your garment photos and to suggest outfits. It receives your photos and written descriptions of your clothes and preferences — never your name, email or account identifier.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app, and it really deletes it — on your device and from our servers, including your images.

2. What we collect, and why

2.1 Account information

When you create an account we collect your email address, password, first name and surname. Your password is never stored in readable form — it is hashed by our authentication provider, and we cannot see it. We use this information solely to create and secure your account, sign you in, and address you by name in the app.

If you sign in with Google. You can create your account with Google instead of choosing a password. When you do, Google tells us your email address and your name, and we store them in your Garmify account exactly as if you had typed them yourself — nothing else is collected, and we never see your Google password. We do not keep any Google credential that would let us reach your Google account or act on your behalf. Google will know that you have signed in to Garmify, and for that part it acts on its own behalf rather than ours, under Google's privacy policy; you can review or withdraw Garmify's access at any time in your Google account settings. Signing in this way is optional — an email address and password work just as well.

If you sign in with Apple. You can also create your account with your Apple ID. Apple tells us your email address and your name, and we store them in your Garmify account in the same way. If you choose Hide My Email, Apple gives us a private relay address instead of your real one, and that relay address is all we ever hold — mail we send to it is forwarded by Apple. Apple provides your name only the very first time you authorise Garmify, which is why the app fills it in at that moment.

There is one thing we store for Apple sign-in that we do not store for Google. Apple requires that deleting your Garmify account also ends Garmify's link to your Apple ID, so it stops appearing under Sign in with Apple in your Apple account. Doing that requires a token Apple issues once, at the moment you sign in, so we keep that token on our server, attached to your account. It is used for exactly one thing — revoking the connection when you delete your account — and for nothing else. It cannot be read back by the app or by anyone signed in to it, it grants no access to your Apple account or its contents, and it is destroyed along with everything else when your account is deleted. Signing in this way is optional; an email address and password work just as well.

2.2 Photos of your clothing

When you add a garment, the photo is sent — through our own server, which forwards it to our AI provider — so the item can be automatically identified (its name, category, colour, pattern, warmth and formality). This is what saves you from typing those details in yourself. We route the request through our server so the provider's key stays private and so we can apply per-user rate limits; the photo passes through for that single purpose and we do not keep the original photo.

The app then keeps a cleaned-up, background-removed cut-out of the garment. That cut-out image is stored on your device and backed up to your private storage area in your Garmify account (hosted by Supabase), so your wardrobe restores when you reinstall or sign in on a new device. Only you can access it. Please avoid photographing people, documents or anything sensitive in the background of your garment photos.

2.3 Details used to suggest outfits

To suggest what to wear, the app sends a short written description of the relevant part of your wardrobe and your preferences to our AI provider (again through our own server, as above). Nothing here identifies you — no name, email or account identifier is included — but you should know what it contains:

  • Descriptions of your garments — name, category, colour, pattern and similar tags. The images themselves are not re-sent.
  • A styling note derived from your gender (for example, "menswear"). If you chose "prefer not to say", nothing is sent.
  • Your age as a number — derived from your date of birth. The AI provider receives only the number, not the date itself. (Your date of birth is stored in your account — see 2.5.)
  • Your occupation, dress code and any planned activities, so the suggestion suits your day.
  • Any event description you type into the day's plan, and trip destinations and dates when building a packing list.
  • The language the app is running in, so suggestions come back written in your language.

Because a free-text event description is sent as you wrote it, please avoid putting anything sensitive or identifying in that field.

2.4 Location

Garmify suggests outfits based on the weather, which requires knowing roughly where you are. If you grant location permission, we take your device's position and deliberately round it to roughly one kilometre before sending it to our weather provider to retrieve the local forecast. Your precise coordinates never leave the app. You may instead search for a city by name, in which case that search term is sent rather than your device location.

Location is used only to fetch weather. Your precise coordinates are never stored — not by us and not by the weather provider — not used to build a profile, and not shared with anyone else. If you set a location by name instead, that city label is saved to your profile and backed up to your account so the app remembers it. You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings; the app remains usable, and you can set your location manually.

2.5 Your wardrobe data, stored in your account

Garmify requires an account, and the wardrobe you build is kept on your device and backed up to your private account (hosted by Supabase) so it restores when you reinstall or sign in on another device. This data is scoped to your account — other users cannot see it, and we do not use it for any purpose other than running the app for you. It includes:

  • Your garment cut-out images, in your private storage area
  • Your date of birth (only your age in years is ever sent to the AI provider, as described above)
  • Your outfit history and record of what you have worn
  • Your saved outfits, weekly plans and packing lists
  • Your profile preferences — gender, occupation, dress code, workdays, chosen location and temperature unit
  • Your subscription status
  • A per-day count of how many AI requests you have made — a number only, kept so we can apply fair-use limits and stop a single account exhausting the service for everyone
  • If, and only if, you signed in with Apple: the single-purpose Apple revocation token described in 2.1

A few things stay only on your device and are never sent to us — for example your notification preferences and the app's local settings.

Deleting your account removes all of the above from our servers, including your images — see "Deleting your account" below.

2.6 Subscription information

If you subscribe to Garmify Pro, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see or receive your payment card, billing address or similar financial details. We only learn whether an active subscription exists.

2.7 App activity and diagnostics

Garmify uses two Google services, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They are treated differently, because they are different things:

  • Crash and error reports (Crashlytics) — always on. When the app crashes, or when something it can recover from fails (tagging a photo, generating an outfit, loading subscription options, removing a background, syncing your wardrobe, or signing in), we receive a technical report: the error, where in the code it happened, and basic device and app information such as the model, operating-system version and app version. These reports contain no photos, no wardrobe contents and nothing you have written; where a report concerns one of your items it names only that item's internal reference, never its picture or description. We rely on them to find and fix faults, and they cannot be switched off separately from using the app.
  • Usage analytics — off unless you opt in. If you agree, we record a short, fixed list of events so we can see which parts of the app are actually used: creating an account, signing in, adding an item, tagging an item, subscribing, restoring purchases, deleting an account, and sending feedback. We record that the action happened — not your photos, garments, outfits or any text you enter.

We ask once, on your first launch inside the app, and either answer is remembered so you are not asked again. Until you answer, nothing is collected. You can change your mind whenever you like in Profile → Share usage data; switching it off stops collection and asks Google to delete the analytics data held for that installation. Your answer is kept on the device it was given on and is deliberately not copied to your other devices, because consent given on one device is not consent on another.

Both services are linked to your account identifier, so a report can be traced back to the account that hit the problem. Neither is linked to an advertising identifier — Garmify does not use one, and IDFA collection is explicitly disabled in the iOS app — and neither is used to build advertising or marketing profiles.

3. What we do not do

To state it plainly, Garmify does not:

  • include advertising, attribution or cross-app tracking software, or use an advertising identifier
  • collect usage analytics unless you have opted in, or collect anything at all before you have answered
  • put your photos, wardrobe contents or anything you have written into an analytics or crash report
  • track your behaviour across other apps or websites
  • build advertising profiles or infer interests for marketing
  • sell, rent or trade your personal information
  • use your photos to train AI models of our own

6. Legal bases for processing

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region with similar law, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract — to create your account and provide the features you asked for, including garment tagging and outfit suggestions.
  • Consent — for device location, for notifications, and for usage analytics. Garmify's reminders are scheduled by the app on your own device: we operate no push service, and no notification token or device identifier is sent to us. You may withdraw location and notification permission at any time in your device settings, and analytics consent in Profile → Share usage data, without affecting the rest of the app.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the service secure and functioning, to diagnose and fix faults through crash and error reporting, and to respond to your support requests.

7. How long we keep information

  • Account information is kept for as long as your account exists, and is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Your wardrobe data and images backed up to your account are kept until you delete them in the app or delete your account, at which point they are removed from our servers.
  • Photos and descriptions sent to our AI provider are processed transiently to produce tags and suggestions, and are not retained by us. The provider may retain them briefly for abuse-prevention and operational purposes under its own policy.
  • Location data is used in the moment to fetch a forecast and is not retained by us.
  • Crash and error reports are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Usage analytics, if you opted in, are retained by Firebase Analytics for up to 14 months. Withdrawing consent stops collection and asks Google to delete what is held for that installation.
  • The on-device copy of your wardrobe remains on your device until you delete it, sign out, or uninstall the app.

You can delete your account at any time, from inside the app: open Profile → Delete account and confirm. This permanently removes your account and everything associated with it — your profile, your wardrobe data and your stored images — from our servers, and erases the Garmify data held on your device. It cannot be undone. If you would rather we did it for you, email canfatihberat.support@gmail.com from your registered address.

To exercise any other right, contact canfatihberat.support@gmail.com. We will respond within one month.

9. Security

All communication between the app and our providers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS, and your data is encrypted at rest on our provider's servers. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and are never visible to us. On your device, your wardrobe is held in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. In the cloud, your data and images are isolated to your account by row-level security, so no other user can reach them.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affecting your personal data occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authority where the law requires it.

10. Children

Garmify is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 years old to create an account, and if you are under the age of digital consent in your country (which is up to 16 in parts of the European Union) you need your parent or guardian's permission to use it. We do not knowingly collect information from children below these ages. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact canfatihberat.support@gmail.com and we will delete it.
